**Solo Play fits a phone, upright or on its side.** The board takes the width of the screen and the panel the room under it, so the board sizes and the Shake button are no longer pushed off the bottom, and Clear and Done stay at the foot of the panel instead of scrolling away. On a phone the top bar keeps its Back arrow, the screen's name and the grown-ups' button on one line, and the end-of-board sheet scrolls under its heading with Play again and Home kept in view, also at the largest text size. Words such as "finished" are no longer drawn with a broken "fi" in the pixel typeface.
