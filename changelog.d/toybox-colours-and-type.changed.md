**The application has a new palette and typefaces.** The page is sunshine, text and outlines are night purple, panels are cream, and selection is coral; the dark scheme is the same palette on deep purple. Titles, buttons and labels are set in Pixelify Sans, small labels in Silkscreen, and the words a child reads, the dice and every number in Andika. Text is never smaller than 14 pixels. Shapes, shadows and pictures follow in later changes.
