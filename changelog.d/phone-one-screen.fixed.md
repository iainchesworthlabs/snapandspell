**On a phone, Home and Solo Play fit the screen, with nothing to scroll down to.** Home shows one big card at a time, Play first and the grown-ups' card a swipe to the side (the next card's edge is in view), instead of a page that ran off the bottom. In Solo Play the board shrinks a little so that the word in hand, the words found, "Show me a start", "Finish board", Clear and Done are all on the screen at once, and the choice of board and Shake are on the screen before the shake. On a phone on its side the board is a square as high as the screen and the panel beside it holds the words next to the buttons.
