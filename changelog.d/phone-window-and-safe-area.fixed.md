**The app fits a phone's screen.** On an iPhone or an Android phone the window is the screen, in either orientation, and the screens sit inside the notch, the rounded corners and the home indicator. Before, it opened as a 900 pixel desktop window pushed off the right and bottom of the screen. On a narrow screen the "My boards" and "My jewels" buttons on the top bar show only their pictures (a screen reader still says their names), and the Home cards stack. The name under the icon on iOS and macOS is "Snap and Spell", with the spaces.
