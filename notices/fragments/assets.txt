Bundled assets
--------------

- The feedback sounds, assets/sounds/*.wav (tap, rattle, letter, land, cheer,
  tick, chime and cleared), are the project's own: short synthesised tones and
  noise bursts written by tools/sounds/make_sounds.ps1, not third-party audio.
  They are placeholders for the phoneme clips and recorded cheers that follow
  the literacy review (docs/design/phonics.md); the files are owned by the
  project and licensed with it. They are compiled into the application's
  resources as :/sounds/<name>.wav, so no file of them is installed.

- The board scene's light probe, src/app/qml/scene/lightprobe/sky.ktx, is the
  project's own: it is baked by tools/lightprobe from the sky the scene would
  otherwise make at run time, and it is not third-party artwork. It is compiled
  into the application's resources, so no file of it is installed.

- No font is bundled. The screens ask for Andika, which is not shipped, and
  fall back through the platform's own fonts per glyph. Andika (SIL Open Font
  License) will be added with its licence text and glyph check before it ships
  (docs/licensing.md, "Fonts and the interface's languages"), and no CJK face
  is bundled.
