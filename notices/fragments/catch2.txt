Catch2
------

The project's tests use Catch2, under the Boost Software License 1.0.

Catch2 is not in a package: no test program is installed, and the two programs
do not link it. It is fetched by the build (the vcpkg "tests" feature) and used
only while the tree is built and tested. Its licence text travels with its
source and with the vcpkg port's installed copyright file.
