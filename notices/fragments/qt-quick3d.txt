Qt Quick 3D {{QT_VERSION}}
---------------------------

WordJewelsSolver's board is drawn with Qt Quick 3D {{QT_VERSION}}
(Qt6Quick3D, Qt6Quick3DRuntimeRender, Qt6Quick3DUtils and the QtQuick3D QML
module). The Windows package includes them with the rest of Qt; a package that
leaves Qt to the system loads the distribution's copy.
