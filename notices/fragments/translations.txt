The interface's translations
----------------------------

The eight Qt Linguist catalogues in apps/app/translations/ (wjs_en.ts, wjs_fr.ts, wjs_es.ts,
wjs_de.ts, wjs_yi.ts, wjs_he.ts, wjs_zh.ts and wjs_ja.ts) are this project's own
text, under the project's licence, the same as the English wording they
translate. No third-party translation file is used, and no Qt translation
catalogue is installed or merged: the application's own catalogue is embedded
in it.

lupdate and lrelease, Qt's tools that write and compile the catalogues, are
build tools under GPL-3.0 with Qt's exception; their output is the project's.
