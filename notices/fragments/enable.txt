The English word list: ENABLE
-----------------------------

dictionaries/en.txt is ENABLE, the Enhanced North American Benchmark Lexicon
(enable1.txt), 172,823 words, kept unmodified. Its SHA-256 is
3F16130220645692ED49C7134E24A18504C2CA55B3C012F7290E3E77C63B1A89; where it was
fetched from, when, and how the hash was taken are recorded in data/README.md
in the source.

Its originators released it into the public domain: anyone is free to use or
distribute it with no fee, registration or credit required. It is a separate
work from this program and is not covered by the GPL. The public-domain
statement is the one published with the list; it has not been independently
verified, and the chain of title is not recorded in this repository
(docs/licensing.md, "The word list").

The dice in dice/ are the project's own work, made from this list by
tools/data/make_dice.ps1, and data/README.md records the method.
